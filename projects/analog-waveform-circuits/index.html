PROJECT NOTES

Analog Filters & Oscillators

An electronics project investigating how passive filters and active circuits shape periodic signals, through signal conditioning and oscillator experiments.

01

Filtering experiments

Applied RC low-pass filtering to triangle and square wave inputs, including 250 Hz test signals. Compared the output of a single RC stage with cascaded stages to observe how filtering changes waveform shape and amplitude.

02

Active waveform generation

Built an op-amp oscillator circuit to explore square, triangle, and sinusoidal-like waveforms. Used an oscilloscope to inspect the signals and a signal generator to drive the filtering experiments.

03

Engineering takeaways

  • Related component values and cutoff frequency to observed waveform behavior.
  • Compared smoothing and attenuation across filtering configurations.
  • Connected circuit theory to breadboard implementation and bench measurements.
