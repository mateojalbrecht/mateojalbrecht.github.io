PROJECT NOTES

HomeKit Motorized Blinds

A hands-on home automation project connecting an ESP32, motor hardware, and a custom mechanical coupling to the tilt rod of window blinds. HomeSpan provides the connection to Apple HomeKit.

01

Hardware and software together

Adapted HomeSpan's window-covering examples for the blinds mechanism and explored servo and motor-driver approaches. The prototype combines a breadboarded controller with custom parts that connect the motor shaft to the blinds.

02

Mechanical iteration

Designed and adjusted a 3D-printed connector for the motor and tilt rod. The part makes the interface between the electronics and the existing blinds tangible, and allows the mechanism to be removed or changed during prototyping.

03

What this demonstrates

  • Adapting embedded firmware to a physical mechanism.
  • Integrating an ESP32 device into Apple HomeKit through HomeSpan.
  • Combining circuit prototyping, motor control, and CAD in a working home automation project.
